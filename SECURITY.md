# Security

This is an orientation library for people inheriting OT environments. No code to exploit, and two things worth reporting fast, one of them more serious here than anywhere else on this account.

**Guidance that could get somebody hurt or a line stopped.** People act on what they read, and in this domain a wrong sentence has physical consequences. If anything here would lead an IT person to touch something they should not, scan something fragile, or misread a safety boundary, open an issue immediately, it outranks everything else in the queue.

**Anything site-identifiable or attack-enabling.** Nothing here should describe a real facility or help someone target one. If you spot either, do not open a public issue. Use GitHub's private vulnerability report on this repo, or the contact on [my profile](https://github.com/HarrisonWard), and it gets handled quietly and quickly.

No bounty program. The reward is an IT person somewhere asking before touching, which is the whole point of the library.
